NOT-FOR-PROFIT LAW · AUCKLAND

Legal advice and support for your not-for-profit

Silcock Law is Jo Silcock, sole practitioner, with over 20 years legal experience including personal and professional expertise working with not-for-profits — Jo can support your organisation to achieve positive change.

Get in touch View services

Not-for-profits play a crucial role in society yet have different considerations, priorities and concerns from commercial organisations. Having worked for and within not-for-profit organisations for fifteen years, Jo appreciates the challenges of the not-for-profit sector.

Legal advice isn’t about scrambling for a solution when something goes wrong. Good advice can save your not-for-profit time and money — not just by avoiding problems, but by adding efficiencies, managing risk and maximising opportunities so people can get on with what they’re there to do: make life better for others.

That is where Silcock Law can help. Jo can understand what your not-for-profit wants to achieve and has the knowledge to help best achieve it — relevant, effective and practical advice that ensures the limited not-for-profit dollar is spent in the most efficient way.
