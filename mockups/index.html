NOT-FOR-PROFIT LAW · AUCKLAND

Legal advice and support for your not-for-profit.

Silcock Law is Jo Silcock, sole practitioner, with personal and professional experience and expertise working with and for not-for-profits to ensure you continue to achieve the positive change you want to see.

Get in touch View services

NPOs play a crucial role in society. What was right for your organisation five — even one — years ago may be different from what is needed now.

Legal advice isn’t about scrambling for a solution when something goes wrong. Good advice can save your NPO time and money — not just by avoiding problems, but by adding efficiencies, managing risk and maximising opportunities so people can get on with what they’re there to do: make life better for others.

That is where Silcock Law can help. Silcock Law understands what your NPO wants to achieve and has the knowledge to help best achieve it — relevant, effective and practical advice that ensures the limited NPO dollar is spent in the most efficient way.

SERVICES

How Silcock Law can help

Not-for-profits play a crucial role in society yet have different considerations, priorities and concerns from commercial organisations. Having worked for and within not-for-profit organisations for fifteen years I appreciate the challenges of the not-for-profit sector.

Jo’s experience and expertise spans the areas not-for-profits most often need support.

Governance

  • Not-for-profit status
  • Constitution, rules and meetings

Compliance

  • Dealing with regulators
  • Submissions

Commercial

  • Intellectual property
  • Contract negotiation & drafting
  • Dispute resolution

Employment

  • Employer obligations

Risk Management

  • Managing information & documents

  • Contracts & financial authorities
